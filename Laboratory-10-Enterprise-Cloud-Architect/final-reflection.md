# Final Reflection – CCM101 Cloud Computing

Completing the ten laboratory activities in CCM101 – Cloud Computing has been a valuable learning experience that strengthened my understanding of cloud computing, Linux system administration, networking, containerization, security, and automation. Throughout this journey, I progressed from learning basic Linux commands to understanding how different infrastructure components work together. I gained practical experience using Docker and Docker Compose, managing persistent storage, deploying multi-tier applications, configuring firewalls using UFW, and creating Bash scripts with scheduled tasks through Cron. These activities helped me understand the importance of building reliable, secure, and maintainable cloud environments.

One of the biggest challenges I encountered was troubleshooting technical problems during implementation. There were situations where commands did not work as expected, configurations needed adjustments, and certain concepts were difficult to understand as a beginner. However, these experiences taught me to be patient, read error messages carefully, investigate problems, and find appropriate solutions. The earlier laboratory activities also helped prepare me for the final mission, where I needed to apply my accumulated knowledge more independently instead of relying entirely on step-by-step instructions.

This course also helped me recognize the importance of cloud computing in real-world IT operations. Organizations rely on secure infrastructure, persistent storage, automated backups, and properly configured networks to maintain their services and protect important data. The skills I developed through these activities provide a foundation for understanding how cloud engineers and system administrators deploy, secure, and maintain infrastructure. I also realized that proper documentation and automation are essential for making systems easier to manage and maintain.

Overall, CCM101 has helped me grow both technically and personally. Although I still have many things to learn, I am more familiar with Linux environments, containerized applications, networking, security configurations, and basic cloud infrastructure management. From being a beginner learning fundamental commands to completing an enterprise cloud architecture project, this journey taught me that learning technology requires continuous practice, patience, and problem-solving. I consider this course an important foundation for my future as an Information Technology professional, and I look forward to developing my skills further in cloud computing, system administration, and DevOps.
